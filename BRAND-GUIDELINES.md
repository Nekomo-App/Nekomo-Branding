# Nekomo brand guidelines

## Brand overview
Nekomo is a community-driven anime and manga application. Its visual identity should be recognizable, modern, readable, and consistent across mobile, Android TV, web, and community platforms.

## Core principles
- **Recognizable:** use consistent marks and naming.
- **Simple:** avoid unnecessary effects and clutter.
- **Readable:** preserve contrast and legibility at small sizes.
- **Consistent:** use approved colors, spacing, and typography.
- **Flexible:** support dark and light backgrounds where practical.

## Logo usage
- Prefer original vector or highest-quality source artwork.
- Preserve proportions; do not stretch, skew, or redraw the mark.
- Maintain clear space around the logo.
- Use approved variants with sufficient contrast.
- Do not add effects unless they are part of an approved variant.

## App icons
- Keep the symbol centered and legible at small sizes.
- Keep important details within crop-safe areas.
- Test Android adaptive icon masks and launcher backgrounds.
- Avoid unnecessary text in the icon.

Concepts and experiments must be labeled as drafts until approved by maintainers.
